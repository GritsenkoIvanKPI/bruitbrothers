# Client Brief — Bruit Brothers

**Client:** Bruit Brothers — Integrated Brand Growth Agency (8 years).
**Audience:** FMCG brand owners and marketing leads, incl. regulated categories (Food & Beverage, Alcohol, Tobacco, OTC Medicines).
**Deliverable:** one-page landing site. Primary action: "Book a free brand audit".

## Copy
Source of truth: `Копия Bruit Brothers.txt` — use all of it, verbatim.

## Visual rules
- Layout reference: `ref.png` (Swiss grid, thin vertical rules, oversized type, blue + off-white).
- Colours (only two): Blue `#15209f`, White `#f0ede5`.
- Fonts (only two, Typekit `rix1cmb`): Meno Display Condensed (display) + Host Grotesk (text).
- Minimal, clean lines, no visual clutter, only meaningful animation. No stock-marketing graphics (arrows, rockets, etc).
- All images use the dither effect from `dither-hover.js`.
- Services: everything visible without clicks; each service ≥ 2/3 of the viewport.

## Concept
"Bruit" is French for noise. Every image starts as two-colour noise (dither) and resolves into the real picture under the cursor — the agency turns noise into the obvious choice. The hero's focus window (sharp eyes in a blurred face) is the shopper's attention at the shelf.
